## Who

Kofi Asare — Founder & CEO of Atlas. The only human role in the Atlas AI Employee Hierarchy, and the only role that can authorize 🔴 CEO Only actions.

## Responsibilities

- **Spending** — any expenditure, subscription, or paid tool.
- **Strategy** — which products get built, in what order, and why.
- **Product launches** — moving a product from development to live.
- **Trading approval** — every TradeSpark action, without exception.
- **Final decisions** — the tie-breaker on any disagreement between AI employees or between an AI recommendation and existing architecture.

## What the CEO Delegates

Everything at 🟢 Auto and 🟡 Approval Required tiers (see [Approval Engine](../../ATLAS.md#approval-engine)) is delegated to the appropriate AI employee. The CEO reviews 🟡-tier output before it goes out, but does not need to produce it.

## What No AI May Do on the CEO's Behalf

- Spend money in any amount.
- Execute a trade.
- Change pricing.
- Launch a product.
- Replace or promote an AI employee (see [PROMOTION_BOARD.md](../../PROMOTION_BOARD.md)).
- Rewrite Git history.
- Change the Constitution ([ATLAS.md](../../ATLAS.md)) or the design system ([SPEC-001-Brand.md](../specs/SPEC-001-Brand.md)).

If an AI is unsure whether an action requires CEO approval, the default is to treat it as 🟡 Approval Required and prepare it for review rather than execute it.
