## Product #1 — MapSpark Ghana

**Status:** In active development (this repository). Sprint 2 CRM Foundation underway.
**Purpose:** Make local businesses easier to discover and easier to buy from — not "we do SEO." See [SPEC-004-MapSpark.md](../specs/SPEC-004-MapSpark.md) for the full product operating specification, three-score model, and evidence rules.
**Services:**
- Visibility Audit
- Google Business Profile Optimization
- Local SEO Improvements
- Monthly Monitoring

## Product #4 — TradeSpark

**Status:** Not started. Phase 4 of the [Roadmap](./ROADMAP.md).
**Purpose:** Internal capital growth.
**Rules:**
- Never launches before business profit is real and sustained.
- Uses strict risk management.
- CEO approval required for every trade — no exceptions, no automation shortcuts (see [Approval Engine](../../ATLAS.md#approval-engine)).

Every product above is built on the same underlying operating system:

- CRM
