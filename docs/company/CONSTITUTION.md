## Amendment process

The Constitution is immutable except by CEO approval. To propose a change:

1. Draft the proposed change and the reasoning behind it.
2. Add a pending entry to [DECISION_LOG.md](../../DECISION_LOG.md) describing the change and marking CEO Approval as "Pending."
3. Do not edit `ATLAS.md` until that entry is marked "Approved."
